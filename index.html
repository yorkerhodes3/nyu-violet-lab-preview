Shared challenge areas

Big enough for NYU. Focused into two days.

Challenge areas help people find common ground without prescribing the solution. Every project starts with a real person, place, or process in mind.

What am I getting into?

Four moments. One useful prototype.

The experience is designed to make starting easy, teamwork visible, and outcomes concrete—even if the prototype is a service or process, not an app.

  1. 01
    Before Sept 14

    Discover

    Post a spark, browse open teams, and make a low-pressure introduction.

  2. 02
    Mon · 9:00 AM

    Frame

    Meet your team, define the person you are helping, and choose a testable outcome.

  3. 03
    Mon 11:00 AM–5:00 PM

    Build + learn

    Prototype in short loops with access to makers, mentors, studios, and subject experts.

  4. 04
    Tue · 3:00–5:00 PM

    Share the signal

    Show what changed, what you learned, and the next smallest step worth taking.
